Our Technical Partner

Technical & EPC Implementation Partnership

Connecting agricultural opportunities with trusted implementation expertise.

Zyro Agri Tech Solutions, an agricultural business development vertical of Zyro International, connects farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), and agricultural stakeholders with suitable protected cultivation and greenhouse farming solutions.

Our work focuses on farmer acquisition, project identification, business development, stakeholder coordination, and market development. Pujya Agritech is our technical and EPC implementation partner, supporting technical planning and project implementation within the agreed project scope.

Through this partnership, we aim to help farmers and FPOs explore suitable agricultural infrastructure, including polyhouses, net houses, and related protected cultivation solutions, based on project feasibility and applicable requirements.

Our Partnership Model

  • Farmer & FPO Engagement: Identify interested farmers, farmer groups, and agricultural development opportunities.
  • Business Development: Qualify potential projects and coordinate stakeholder discussions.
  • Technical & EPC Implementation: Coordinate with Pujya Agritech for technical evaluation, project planning, and implementation within the agreed scope.
  • Project Coordination: Facilitate communication between farmers, FPOs, technical teams, and relevant stakeholders.
  • Solution-Oriented Approach: Explore suitable systems based on land, water availability, crop requirements, budget, and project feasibility.

Our Commitment

We aim to create a transparent, coordinated, and farmer-focused pathway from opportunity identification to technical project implementation, with clearly defined responsibilities and commercially agreed terms.
