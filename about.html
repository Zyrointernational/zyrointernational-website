About

Connecting farmers, projects, suppliers and markets.

Zyro International is being structured as a focused business platform with specialist verticals rather than a single generic services catalogue.

Our positioning

Project development + sourcing.

Zyro Agri Tech Solutions is positioned around agricultural project development, protected cultivation, farm infrastructure, EPC coordination and market-linked project opportunities.

Zyro Global Sourcing & Trading is positioned around requirement-led sourcing for IT parts, industrial equipment and processing units.

Operating principles

  • Start with a qualified requirement.
  • Use specialist partners where technical depth is required.
  • Keep commercial scope and responsibilities clear.
  • Build repeatable project and sourcing workflows.
  • Focus on execution and long-term relationships.
